From the Cristo family

Chosen by people who grow it

Alejandro Cristo brings over 50 years of experience in coffee farming. His daughter Sarah, our CEO, holds a master's degree in agribusiness from Cornell University. Together they blend traditional methods with modern innovation, and that is the standard every club coffee is chosen by.

Our coffee comes from the regions that give Salvadoran coffee its character, from the high-altitude farms of Santa Ana to the volcanic soils of Usulután.
