Alejandro Cristo

Discover the Heart of El Salvador in Every Cup

Born and raised in the coffee-growing regions of El Salvador, Alejandro Cristo shares a deep connection with coffee cultivation that spans generations. His vision was simple yet profound: to bring the authentic taste of El Salvador’s finest coffee to the world while ensuring sustainable farming practices and a fair trade relationship with local farmers.

The Founders

Sarah and Alejandro Cristo

Sarah Cristo, the visionary CEO of Cristo Republic Coffee, carries forward the legacy of deep-rooted family wisdom. Born into a family of coffee farmers in El Salvador, Sarah pursued her education in Cornell University’s College of Agriculture and Life Sciences, renowned as one of the top agricultural programs in the country. With a master’s degree in agribusiness, she honed her expertise in sustainable agriculture and global trade. Alejandro Cristo, her father, is the heart of Cristo Republic Coffee. With over 50 years of experience in coffee farming, Alejandro brings a wealth of knowledge and passion to the company. Together, Sarah and Alejandro have revitalized the Cristo family legacy, blending traditional methods with modern innovation to create exceptional coffee while honoring their heritage and the land that sustains it.
